# FOSS Club: 90-Day Guide



## Overview

| Week | Focus | Key Deliverable |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Week 1-2 | Setup and planning | Club group chat, wiki page live and forum post live |
| Week 3-4 | Inaugural event | Introduce members to Digital Commons |
| Week 5-8 | Follow-up events based on interests |  Thematic hands-on events |
| Week 8-12 | Highlight members and contributions, Q2 planning | Demo day; Q2 roadmap published |

---

*As you navigate this journey, please feel free to reach out to us at [clubs@fossunited.org](mailto:clubs@fossunited.org). We would love to sit down to brainstorm with you, or be an enabler in developing an open source ecosystem in your organisation.*
