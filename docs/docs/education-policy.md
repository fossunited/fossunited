# Policy Tailwinds

If your institution is government-funded or government-aided, the following national policies are relevant. Private institutions may also find them useful as reference material when making the case to leadership.

**Government of India Open Source Policy (2015):** This policy mandates that all central government organisations adopt OSS as the preferred option for new applications and new versions of existing systems. Source code must be available to study, modify, and redistribute without royalty. Full text at sflc.in/policies-and-cases.

**National Education Policy 2020:** NEP 2020 calls explicitly for EdTech tools to be hosted on open source development platforms and emphasises technology integration across curriculum and teacher training. It also mandates credit-based flexibility and encourages multi-disciplinary learning, both compatible with integrating open source contribution as an assessed activity.

**Kerala KITE:** Kerala's KITE program migrated over 2 lakh computers in 14,000 government schools to a custom Ubuntu-based Linux distribution. The estimated cost avoidance is Rs 3,000 crore.<sup>[3]</sup> The program includes customised FOSS educational software built into the OS and aligned to the state curriculum. It is the most comprehensive state-level FOSS-in-education implementation in India and a practical reference for what institutional adoption looks like at scale.

**NPTEL and SWAYAM:** The National Program on Technology Enhanced Learning, run by IIT Madras and six other IITs, has produced over 700 courses available under open access terms.<sup>[5]</sup> SWAYAM provides the national MOOC infrastructure. Institutions can directly integrate NPTEL content into their curriculum under existing open licensing terms.

> **For AICTE-affiliated institutions:** AICTE has encouraged the use of open source tools and platforms in engineering curricula. Integrating FOSS into your curriculum and documenting it can be mentioned in accreditation applications as evidence of technology integration and industry alignment.
