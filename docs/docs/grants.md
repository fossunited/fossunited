# Apply for FOSS Grant

Applying for a FOSS Grant through FOSS United is a process that supports the development and use of Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) in India. Here's how you can apply:

### Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for a grant, you must meet the following criteria:

* Be an individual or organization working on a FOSS project in India.
* Have a clear project plan that outlines the goals of the project and how the funds will be used.
* Be able to demonstrate a need for funding.

### Funding Amounts

The maximum amount of funding available for each grant is ₹6 lakh.

### Co-Sponsored Grants

FOSS United allows organizations to co-sponsor specific FOSS projects and grants by providing a framework for fund disbursal and project management. Co-sponsored grants are executed with the combined approval of FOSS United and the grantee.

### Recent Grants

FOSS United has provided grants to various projects, including Rethink DNS, FireFiles, TinkerHub Foundation, Parseable, and more.

By offering these grants, FOSS United aims to foster the growth and development of the FOSS community in India, supporting projects that align with its mission and values.

Apply [here](https://fossunited.org/grants)
