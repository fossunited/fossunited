# Open Source Related Activities for Non-STEM Branches

Our team worked on this list as part of [Youth Dreamers Foundation's](https://ydfindia.org/) work to support colleges (STEM and Non-STEM) in Bihar.


| Department | Beginner tasks | Tools/devices needed |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising, Sales Promotion and Sales Management | 1) Create open-source marketing templates (campaign briefs, content calendars).<br><br>2) Digitise and annotate public domain advertising archives to build open datasets. | Phone/computer, free design app (Canva free tier or GIMP), browser |
| Ancient History, Culture & Archaeology | 1) Photograph a nearby monument, mazar or temple (Nalanda, Vikramshila, Vaishali, or one in your own town) and upload it to Wikimedia Commons under a free licence.<br><br>2) Mark a monument or a detail related to it on OpenStreetMap. Use the StreetComplete app or the OpenStreetMap editor, which asks simple questions and needs no map-editing skill. | Smartphone camera, Wikimedia/OpenStreetMap account, StreetComplete app |
| B.Com Accounts | 1) Try out GnuCash.<br><br>2) Map local banks, ATMs and accounting firms on OpenStreetMap.<br><br>3) Read about your state's budget on PRS. | Phone/computer, LibreOffice Calc, browser |
| Bachelor of Business Administration | 1) Document a local shop or Jeevika group's business story and publish it under a free licence.<br><br>2) Clean and share a local market price list in a spreadsheet. Try out GnuCash. | Phone/computer, LibreOffice Calc, browser |
| Bachelor of Business Management | 1) Interview a local entrepreneur and publish an open, sourced write-up. | Phone, browser, LibreOffice |
| Bachelor of Computer Application | 1) Try Scratch, a block-based tool with no typing required, to build a small project celebrating a Bihar festival, and share it on the Scratch community.<br><br>2) Write a hello-world program in your favourite programming language. | Computer/tablet, browser, free Scratch/GitHub accounts |
| Bachelor of Library & Information Science | 1) Add your college or local library's book list to Open Library.<br><br>2) Scan and upload a public domain Bihar-related old book to Wikisource or the Internet Archive. | Phone scanning app or scanner, browser, Wikimedia account |
| Bachelor of Tourism & Travel Management | 1) Write or expand the Wikivoyage guide for your town or a nearby heritage site with practical tips.<br><br>2) Mark local hotels, dhabas or bus stands on OpenStreetMap with StreetComplete.<br><br>3) Upload good tourism photos to Wikimedia Commons. | Smartphone with GPS, camera, StreetComplete app, Wikimedia account |
| Bengali | 1) Document local stories in Bengali.<br><br>2) Record a few Bengali sentences for Common Voice. | Browser, on-screen Bengali keyboard, Common Voice/Wikimedia accounts |
| Biology | 1) Photograph a plant, bird or insect near your home and upload it to iNaturalist, adding one record to Bihar's biodiversity map. Or just visit iNaturalist and understand how it works.<br><br>2) Upload a clear photo of a common local species to Wikimedia Commons and link it from its Wikipedia article. | Smartphone camera, iNaturalist app, Wikimedia account |
| Biotechnology | 1) Photograph a local crop pest or disease sample for Wikimedia Commons. | Smartphone, browser, Wikimedia account |
| Botany | 1) Photograph local flora (litchi, sal, mango or banyan) and upload it to iNaturalist or Wikimedia Commons.<br><br>2) List the plants in your college garden in a LibreOffice spreadsheet and share it publicly. | Smartphone camera, iNaturalist app, LibreOffice Calc |
| Chemistry | 1) Photograph common lab equipment and release the images on Wikimedia Commons for other students to use.<br><br>2) Publish your syllabus publicly. | Phone, browser, Wikimedia account |
| Economics (Rural & General) | 1) Add current Bihar facts (per capita income, main crops, Jeevika coverage) to Wikipedia with a citation to the Economic Survey of Bihar.<br><br>2) Clean a village-level government dataset from [data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in), CityFinance or Data For India in a spreadsheet, correcting spellings and blanks.<br><br>3) Mark your local haat or mandi on OpenStreetMap.<br><br>4) Build and publish open economic visualisations using [rawgraphs.io](https://rawgraphs.io). | Computer, LibreOffice Calc, browser, Wikimedia/OpenStreetMap/RAWGraphs account |
| Education | 1) Translate a children's storybook into Hindi, Maithili or Bhojpuri on StoryWeaver.<br><br>2) Publish your college's curriculum online on GitHub or a wiki. | |
| Environment and Water Management | 1) Map ponds, handpumps and drains or other features in your village on OpenStreetMap with StreetComplete or other relevant tools, useful given Bihar's flood and groundwater concerns.<br><br>2) Write a guide on water conservation for your local community using LibreOffice. | Smartphone with GPS, StreetComplete app, browser, LibreOffice |
| Environmental Science | 1) Record a wildlife or plant sighting near you on iNaturalist.<br><br>2) Log a littering or waste hotspot with a geo-tagged photo on OpenLitterMap. | Smartphone with GPS, iNaturalist/OpenLitterMap apps and accounts |
| Geography | 1) Map your village's roads, ponds or embankments on OpenStreetMap with StreetComplete, which helps flood planning along the Ganga or Kosi.<br><br>2) Photograph and geo-tag a local landmark for Wikimedia Commons.<br><br>3) Check out projects on CoRE Stack. | Smartphone with GPS, StreetComplete app, Wikimedia account |
| Hindi | 1) Proofread an old Bihar-related book on Hindi Wikisource.<br><br>2) Translate a children's storybook into Hindi on StoryWeaver, openly licensed for any child to read. | Browser, Wikimedia/StoryWeaver accounts |
| History | 1) Photograph a historical plaque or site and upload it to Wikimedia Commons.<br><br>2) Write about your visit to a historical site in your regional language and post it on your wiki. | Phone camera/scanner, browser, Wikimedia account |
| Home Science | 1) Add a Bihar packaged food (sattu, thekua, litti mix) to Open Food Facts by scanning the barcode and photographing the label.<br><br>2) Write up a family recipe such as litti chokha or dahi chuda with photos for the Wikibooks Cookbook or your college wiki, openly licensed.<br><br>3) Digitise and upload vintage textile patterns to the Internet Archive or Wikimedia Commons. | Smartphone camera, Open Food Facts app, Wikimedia account |
| IT | 1) Write a short beginner's how-to guide for a free and open source tool you learned, and publish it openly. | Computer, browser, LibreOffice |
| Legal Studies | 1) Use OpenStreetMap to map local public notaries, legal aid clinics, courthouses and police stations.<br><br>2) Read about your state's budget on PRS.<br><br>3) Learn about RTIs. | Browser, Wikimedia account |
| Maithili | 1) Record a Maithili folk song or sentence for Common Voice.<br><br>2) Write a local story in Maithili and post it on your college wiki. | Phone/computer, Devanagari keyboard, browser, Common Voice/Wikimedia accounts |
| Mathematics | 1) Try out PhET simulations.<br><br>2) Draw a geometric figure in GeoGebra, a free browser tool, and release it on Wikimedia Commons. | Phone/computer, GeoGebra (browser-based), Wikimedia account |
| Music | 1) Record a Chhath geet or Sama-Chakeva song and upload it to Wikimedia Commons under a free licence. | Phone with mic, browser, Wikimedia account |
| Personnel Management and Industrial Relations | 1) Analyse open datasets on regional wages or labour statistics on Kaggle or other platforms. | Browser, Wikimedia account |
| Physics | 1) Redraw a simple textbook diagram on paper or in a drawing app and release it on Wikimedia Commons for free reuse.<br><br>2) Try out PhET simulations. | Phone/tablet, drawing app or scanner, browser |
| Political Science | 1) Read the Wikipedia article on the Champaran Satyagraha or the JP Movement.<br><br>2) Read about your state's budget on PRS.<br><br>3) Mark your panchayat bhavan or polling booth on OpenStreetMap with StreetComplete. | Smartphone/browser, StreetComplete app |
| Psychology | 1) Add a Hindi or Maithili translation of a basic psychology term to Wiktionary.<br><br>2) Proofread a mental health awareness article on Wikipedia for accuracy and clarity.<br><br>3) Record a few Hindi or Maithili sentences on Mozilla Common Voice, which helps build voice tools for people who cannot read or type. | Phone or computer with mic, browser, Common Voice account |
| Public Administration | 1) Add sourced details of a Bihar scheme (Jal Jeevan Hariyali, Jeevika) to Wikipedia.<br><br>2) Transcribe a page of an old Bihar government gazette on Wikisource.<br><br>3) Mark your block office or panchayat bhavan on OpenStreetMap. | Browser, Wikimedia account, StreetComplete app |
| Sanskrit | 1) Record a short audio pronunciation of common Sanskrit terms for Wikimedia Commons. | Browser, Devanagari keyboard, Wikimedia account |
| Social Science | 1) Help build a survey in Hindi to collect information on social problems in your region. | Smartphone, browser, Formbricks account |
| Sociology | 1) Record a local proverb or saying in Bhojpuri, Maithili or Magahi for Mozilla Common Voice.<br><br>2) Write about a local custom or festival, such as Chhath or Sama-Chakeva. Publish it on your college's wiki page.<br><br>3) Mark a community space (chaupal, akhara, temple) on OpenStreetMap. | Phone with mic, browser, Common Voice/OpenStreetMap accounts |
| Urdu | 1) Record a few Urdu sentences or a couplet's recitation for Common Voice. | Phone/computer, on-screen Urdu keyboard, browser, Common Voice account |
| Zoology | 1) Photograph or record a local bird or animal and upload it with location to iNaturalist.<br><br>2) Add your area's species checklist to your college's wiki.<br><br>3) Record a bird call on your phone and share it on Wikimedia Commons. | Smartphone camera/mic, iNaturalist app |
