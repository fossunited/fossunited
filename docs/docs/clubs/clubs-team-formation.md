# Team Formation Guide

Just like in an open-source project, a lead’s (maintainer’s) job isn't to do *everything* alone; it’s to "maintain" a space where others can easily contribute, learn, and grow.

## Points of Contact (POCs)

For the FOSS United team to effectively support, mentor, and coordinate with your club, we ask that you designate **3 to 5 members** as our primary points of contact. We’ve noticed that smaller teams work the best and would encourage you to do the same.

Typically, this small squad handles:

* **The Anchor:** The main planner for all things the club plans to do
* **The Tech Guide:** The tech superhero that everyone can rely on
* **The Community Voice:** Keeps the community on Discord/Telegram or in-person active and engaged.
* **The Ops Helper:** Handles the "boots on the ground" logistics like venue bookings and event dates.
* **The Faculty Coordinator:** The person helping you navigate the college administrative hurdles.

<mark>**While we let you decide who plays what roles, we require you to have at least one non-male student member in your core team.**</mark>

---

## Growing Your Team

While we ask for **3 to 5 members** as the main POCs for FOSS United, we know that running a big club takes more than that! Feel free to add more people to your extended team as needed.

---

## Ready to start?

Once you've identified your team, introduce them to us by emailing their details to [clubs@fossunited.org](mailto:clubs@fossunited.org)! We’ll get them plugged into the right channels so we can start building together.
