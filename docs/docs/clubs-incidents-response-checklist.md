# Incident Response

Ensuring the Code of Conduct is upheld and respected is a primary responsibility of all Club Leads. This checklist serves as a vital resource to ensure that every reported incident is handled with the utmost care, professional integrity, and the seriousness it deserves.

Please make sure to read through the [FOSS United Code of Conduct](https://fossunited.org/code-of-conduct) thoroughly and ensure that all volunteers are made aware of the reporting procedure prior to the event.

Ensure you include a dedicated slide in your opening ceremony deck that displays the link to the [Code of Conduct](https://fossunited.org/code-of-conduct).


## Incident Response Checklist

- [ ] **Stay Calm:** Take a breath. Your composure sets the tone for the reporter’s safety.
- [ ] **Involve a Trusted Third Person:** If possible, ensure you are not alone with the reporter. Bring in a neutral witness to observe and "break the mandate."
    - This person must not belong to the group being reported.
        - If the report is against an organizer, ask a trusted speaker or sponsor representative to join.
        - If the report is against a sponsor, bring in another organizer or club lead.

---

> “I need a moment of your time for something important. We have had a Code of Conduct incident reported, and as per our protocol, I need a **neutral third person** to sit in as a witness while I take the report. Your role is simply to be present to ensure the process is transparent and fair for everyone. Can you join us in a private space for a few minutes? This must remain strictly confidential.”

---

- [ ] **Introduce the third person**

> Before we begin, I want to introduce [Name]. To ensure that this process is handled with the highest level of integrity and that your safety is prioritized, FOSS United protocol requires a neutral third party to be present. [Name] is here solely as a witness to the conversation. They are not part of the group involved in your report, and they are committed to maintaining your absolute privacy.

---

- [ ] **Find a Safe Space:** Immediately move the reporter to a private, quiet area away from the incident.
- [ ] **Protect Privacy:** Assure them that this stays within the necessary FOSS United team members.
- [ ] **Call the Incident Line:** Dial **[9900982939](tel:+919900982939)** immediately. Tell the reporter that this is standard operating procedure as mentioned in the [Code of Conduct](https://fossunited.org/code-of-conduct). Call twice in a row to bypass "Do Not Disturb" (DND) settings on phones.
    - **Follow Up:** If the call isn't answered after the second attempt, notify the central team (Rahul, Ansh, Siddharth) by calling them directly.
- [ ] **Don’t play the interpreter:** Let the reporter speak to the FOSS United team member.
- [ ] **Action:** Wait for the team’s instructions and handle resolutions accordingly.
