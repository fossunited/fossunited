# FOSS Club Onboarding

If you haven't already, [book a call with our team](https://cal.com/team/fossunited/learners-program) to discuss the FOSS Club program. This will be a good opportunity for us to tell you more about FOSS United and get to know about your journey.

If our team has confirmed your FOSS Club status, please go ahead and complete the following steps to establish a club in your college:

- Obtain a permission letter from your college to start a FOSS Club in your college. Share it with our team at [clubs@fossunited.org](mailto:clubs@fossunited.org)
- Fill out the [FOSS Clubs application](https://fossunited.org/clubs/start-a-club/new).
- Allow the FOSS United team 3-5 days to review your application.
- While you wait to hear from us, go ahead and [form your core team](clubs-team-formation.md), including a faculty coordinator. We **mandate** that each club has at least one non-male core team member.
- Once your club is approved, you'll receive an official letter of confirmation and then it's time to celebrate!!
