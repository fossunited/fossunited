# FOSS United Platform

The **FOSS United Platform** is an open source community management platform built using the [Frappe Framework](https://frappe.io/framework). It serves as the central hub for all FOSS United activities, including event organization, community collaboration, grant tracking, and more.

This platform is part of a larger mission to foster a vibrant Free and Open Source Software (FOSS) ecosystem in India. Whether you're a developer, enthusiast, educator, or industry partner, you're invited to explore, contribute, and grow with the community.

Together, we're building a culture of openness, collaboration, and innovation through FOSS.

## What's on the Platform

- **User Profiles**
  Showcase your contributions, maintain a portfolio, and manage your activities through a personal dashboard.

- **FOSS Communities & Clubs**
  Join or manage local FOSS communities across India.

- **Event Hosting**
  Organize and participate in [community events](https://fossunited.org/events/timeline).

- **Grants and Initiatives**
  Track and apply for community grants, explore ongoing initiatives.

See more on [our initiatives](initiative.md).

## Tech Stack

- **Backend**: Python with Frappe Framework
- **Frontend**: HTML/CSS, Vue.js, Bootstrap v4, TailwindCSS
- **Database**: MariaDB
- **Caching**: Redis

FOSS United relies on a variety of self-hosted services, which you can find listed [in our tech stack](https://fossunited.org/stack)

## Getting Involved

The FOSS United Platform is open to contributions, code, content, and community. You can:

- Join local meetups or events
