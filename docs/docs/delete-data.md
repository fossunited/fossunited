# Delete personal data

If you want us to delete the personal data saved on FOSS United Foundation systems, including Personally Identifiable Information (PII), submit your email address on the [Delete Data](https://fossunited.org/request-for-account-deletion/new) page.

![Request data](./assets/delete-data.png)

## What happens next

1. You'll receive a confirmation email — click the link to confirm your request.
2. Our team reviews it and completes the deletion, usually within a week.
3. Your account is disabled and your public profile is removed — your name, photo, bio, contact and social links, location, and your education / work / project details. Your profile page (`/u/<username>`) will no longer be available.

## Other records

Where you took part in activities (for example event RSVPs or talk proposals), we remove or anonymize your personal details where we can; some records may be kept without identifying you so event history stays accurate.

Financial records such as ticket payments are retained where we're legally required to, and may still contain limited personal details.

> This is handled in stages. If you have a specific concern about where your data appears, mention it in your request and our team will take care of it.
