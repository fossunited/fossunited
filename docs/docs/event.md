# What to do at the event

## General

- Keep your phones and gadgets in Do Not Disturb mode.
- Avoid discussion on topic which can offend others. This includes but not limited to politics, sex and religion.

## For the Speaker

- Prepare well for the meetup - an event is attended by 50-100 people who give their
time for your talk. And the worst which can be given to them is sloppy talks.

## For the Audience

- Questions are usually encouraged at the end of the talk. This does not interrupt
the talks flow.
- Connection with other fellow open-source enthusiasts and contributors, share what you are building/working on and how you are doing it
and receive feedback on the same.
