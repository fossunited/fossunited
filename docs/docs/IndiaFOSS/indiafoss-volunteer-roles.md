# Volunteer Roles

Roles marked \* are generally led by or reserved for the full time team/nominated community members

## On Ground

| Role | Count | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Auditorium Manager / Head (Audi 1 & 2) | 1 per Audi (4) | Oversees all volunteer activity within the auditorium across both days, ensuring sessions run on time and the space is well-managed. |
| Auditorium Volunteer (Audi 1 & 2) | 6 per Audi (by shifts)<br>3 for Audi 2 | Assists with seating, speaker setup, Q&A management, and attendee flow during sessions in the auditoriums. |
| Emcee | 4 (2 per Audi) | Hosts and energises the auditorium by introducing speakers, managing transitions between sessions, and keeping the audience engaged. |
| Registration Desk | 12 (2 Speakers/Guests/Contributor + 4 General each shift) | Manages check-in for all IndiaFOSS attendees. Distributes ID stickers, and redirects entry-related queries to helpdesk.<br>From 8-12 |
| Devroom Overall Coordinator | 1 | Acts as the central point of coordination across all devrooms, ensuring each devroom POC has what they need and escalating issues as needed. |
| Devroom Volunteers | 2 per devroom | Serves as the on-ground point of contact for a specific devroom, liaising with the devroom managers and ensuring sessions run without disruption. |
| Booth Manager | 2+2 | Oversees the project and community booth area, helping exhibitors set up, stay on schedule, and engage with attendees. |
| Sponsor Manager | 3 | Is the primary on-ground contact for sponsors, ensuring their booths, branding, and any commitments are fulfilled during the event. |
| Vendor/Logistics Manager | 2 | Overlook all operations and logistics at the event |
| Vendor - F&B | 2 | Coordinates with food and beverage vendors to ensure meals and refreshments are served on time and in adequate quantity. |
| Vendor - A/V | 2 | Liaises with the audio-visual team to ensure microphones, LED wall, and sound systems are functional across all spaces. |
| Vendor - Photography | 2 | Coordinates with the photography and videography team to ensure key moments, spaces, and sessions are well-documented throughout the event.<br>These volunteers will help Marketing Volunteers too. |
| Vendor - Venue Setup | 1 | Works with the venue team to ensure tables, chairs, and layout are in place and adjusted as needed across both days. |
| Vendor - Accommodation | 2 | Books accommodation for diversity scholars and volunteers and acts as a point of contact for any stay-related issues. |
| Vendor - Printables | 2 | Manages ordering, receipt, quality check, and distribution of printed materials including T-shirts, stickers, badges, and signage. |
| Livestream Volunteer | 5 | Works with Shree to manage stream health, and ensures each auditorium and devroom session is broadcasted live to the online audience. |
| Short form video and photography volunteers | 20 | Edits and processes marketing videos pre-event and session recordings post-event for publication on FOSS United's socials. |
| Merch Manager | 3 | Runs the merchandise counter, manages inventory of T-shirts and FOSS United swag, and handles sale and distribution to attendees. |
| \* Helpdesk / FOSS United Booth | 1 (full-time or GB) | Staffs the FOSS United community booth, answering questions about the organisation, ongoing projects, and how to get involved. |
| Diversity Volunteer | 5 | Supports the diversity and inclusion initiatives at the event, assists scholarship attendees, and helps create a welcoming environment for underrepresented groups. |
| Speaker Manager | 5 (2 for each audi and 1 main manager) | Is the dedicated point of contact for speakers assigned to their auditorium or track, ensuring they are briefed, comfortable, and on stage on time.<br>Capturing photos and getting quotes from speakers for live tweeting (2). |
| Code of Conduct (CoC) Volunteer | Foundation Members | Monitors the event for CoC violations, receives and escalates reports, and helps ensure a safe and inclusive environment for all attendees. |
| First Floor Manager/ Attendee Manager | 1 (full-time) | Oversees all volunteer activity and attendee flow on the first floor, acting as the go-to person for any issues in that space.<br>Monitors overall attendee experience on the ground, proactively addressing confusion, crowding, or accessibility needs. |
| Ground Floor Manager/ Attendee Manager | 1 (full-time) | Oversees all volunteer activity and attendee flow on the ground floor, ensuring smooth operations across registration, booths, and common areas.<br>Monitors overall attendee experience on the ground, proactively addressing confusion, crowding, or accessibility needs. |
| Marketing Volunteer (rotating) | 3 | Captures real-time content during the event - quotes, photos, moments - for social media posts and live coverage.<br>2 for live-tweeting, 1 for short-clips. |
| Low Waste Volunteer | ~4 | Make sure waste from the event is handled properly. You will work with a third party vendor for this. |
| Floating Volunteer | 6 (3 per floor) | Steps in wherever needed across the floor, filling gaps, supporting other teams, and handling ad-hoc tasks as they come up. |
| Other Smaller rooms | One each | We’ve historically had at least 3 small rooms for BoFs |
| Network Volunteer | 10 | Handle internet and Wifi for IndiaFOSS. |
