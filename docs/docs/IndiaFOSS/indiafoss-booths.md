## Checklist

Here's a checklist for you to follow throughout the event:

### Before the day / Team arrival

- [ ] Booth manager ensures the full booth volunteer team arrives on time, before exhibitors start setting up
- [ ] Confirm roles for the day: exhibitor check-in, covering each floor or zone (including the hardware booths), crowd flow near busy booths
- [ ] Walk the floors with the floor maps and confirm every booth spot is numbered/labelled and matches the plan
- [ ] Map out exit points, power points, and volunteer placements on each floor
- [ ] Booth manager checks in with the venue/AV team to confirm power, Wi-Fi, and extension boards are in place

### Booth setup

- [ ] Confirm each booth has its table, chairs, name board/signage, and power access as allotted
- [ ] Hardware booths have enough power sockets and safe cable routing (no trip hazards across walkways)
- [ ] Booths tagged "Display" on the booth listing have their display/screen set up and tested before doors open
- [ ] Coordinate with the volunteer coordinators for available volunteers
- [ ] Community partner booths are set up and their signage clearly shows them as community partners
- [ ] Check that walkways between booths are clear and wide enough for crowd flow

### Exhibitor check-in (ongoing, through the morning)

- [ ] Tick off each booth team as it arrives against the day's booth list
- [ ] Guide each team to their spot using the floor map
- [ ] Share the key contacts with each team: booth manager, AV/tech support, and help desk
- [ ] Brief each team on timings: booth open/close times, lunch, and when they can leave the booth unattended
- [ ] If a booth team hasn't arrived 30 minutes before doors open, contact them right away and flag it to the booth coordination lead

### During the day

- [ ] Volunteers do regular rounds of the booth floors to check whether any booth needs help
- [ ] Watch for crowding near popular booths and redirect foot traffic so walkways stay clear
- [ ] Help booths that are short-staffed cover lunch breaks, or make sure they're not left unattended with equipment
- [ ] Keep an eye on hardware demos for safety (hot soldering irons, exposed wiring, batteries)
- [ ] Nudge attendees towards booth areas that are easy to miss, such as a hardware area away from the talk halls

### Day 1 → Day 2 changeover (multi-day events)

- [ ] Confirm Day 1-only booths know they must clear their spot by end of Day 1
- [ ] Confirm Day 2-only booths know when they can set up on Day 2 and where their spot is
- [ ] Swap signage/name boards for the Day 2-only booths
- [ ] Remind both-day booths about overnight rules: what can be left behind, and whether the venue is secured
- [ ] Confirm the exhibit day for any booth with no day listed on the booth page

### End of day / teardown

- [ ] Give booths a 30-minute and a 10-minute warning before the floor closes
- [ ] Check every booth spot is cleared (or secured, for both-day booths) and no equipment or trash is left behind
- [ ] Collect any borrowed items (extension boards, displays, signage) from booths
- [ ] Do a final walk of all booth floors before signing off with the venue team

### If something goes wrong

- [ ] Booth team no-show → inform the booth coordination lead and decide whether the spot stays empty or gets reassigned
- [ ] Power/display/Wi-Fi failure → flag to the venue/AV team immediately
- [ ] Safety issue at a hardware booth (overheating, burning smell, exposed wires) → cut power to that booth and inform the venue team
- [ ] Complaint about a booth, or a Code of Conduct issue → escalate to the organising team/CoC response team right away
- [ ] Lost/damaged equipment → note the booth, the item, and the time, and inform the booth coordination lead

### General

- [ ] Keep a copy of the floor maps and the booth lists for each day on hand (printed or digital) at all times
- [ ] Keep each booth's point-of-contact name and number handy
- [ ] Keep the venue/AV team and booth coordination lead contacts handy for quick reach
