IndiaFOSS is one of the largest gatherings for the country's Free and Open Source Software communities. Less of a classical conference, more of a festival for FOSS, digital commons and all the people who make it happen!

Whether you are a seasoned developer, an aspiring enthusiast, or someone with a passion for technology, IndiaFOSS invites you to be part of this vibrant community.
