FLOSS FUND

The grants program was initiated with the vision to provide financial support to FOSS projects and events across the country.

If you have a FOSS specific project or initiative and in need of monetary support, you can start a conversation with us at grants@fossunited.org.
We fund FOSS projects by raising funds from the tech industry, either through our Industry Partnership Program or by reaching out to them upon receiving requests from FOSS projects/organizations.

Our grants program also provide financial support to FOSS events. To avail an event grant, write to us with a proposal on the event specifics like goals, target audience, support links and documents etc.
